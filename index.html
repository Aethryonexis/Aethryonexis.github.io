02 / Why us — what you actually get

Why hand it to us.

Most teams get one of two things: a shop that builds fast and leaves you a mess, or a consultancy that writes a document and never ships. We do the whole thing, and we hand it back so you can run it without us.

01

You own everything

Documented, reproducible, and yours to run. No proprietary lock-in, no dependency on us to keep the lights on. If you never call us again, everything still works.

02

Security is not an add-on

Threat modelling and applied cryptography are part of how we build, not a phase we bolt on at the end. Signed payloads, race-safe writes, and real auth on day one.

03

Deliberately small

You talk to the engineer building your system — not an account manager relaying messages. Fewer people, less translation loss, faster decisions.

04

We ship in slices

Nothing sits unmerged for a month. You see working software early and often, which means you can change your mind before it gets expensive.

05

Built to survive review

Tests, failure modes, and observability are part of the deliverable. When your investor's technical advisor opens the repo, it holds up.

06

Straight answers

If your idea has a fatal flaw, we say so before you pay us to build it. If something is cheaper to buy than build, we tell you that too.

04 / Process — scope to hand off

A rhythm, not a ritual.

Five phases, every engagement. You always know which one you're in, what comes out of it, and what it costs you to change your mind.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Understand the system before touching it. We map what exists, what actually hurts, and what success looks like in measurable terms.

    Deliverable Written scope, constraints, and a fixed estimate.

  2. 02

    Architect

    Decide the tradeoffs explicitly and write them down — what we optimise for, what we deliberately give up, and the failure modes we accept.

    Deliverable Architecture doc, data model, threat model.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship in slices. Nothing sits unmerged for a month, so you see working software early and can redirect before it gets expensive.

    Deliverable Working increments, weekly, in your repo.

  4. 04

    Harden

    Tests, threat model verification, failure modes, and observability. We try to break it on purpose before real traffic does it for us.

    Deliverable Test suite, monitoring, incident runbook.

  5. 05

    Hand off

    Documented, reproducible, and yours to run. We walk your team through it until they can operate and extend it without us.

    Deliverable Docs, walkthrough, full ownership.

How we verify every build

  • Automated testsUnit, integration, and end-to-end suites that run on every commit — not written once at the end.
  • Code reviewEvery change is reviewed before it merges. No direct pushes to production branches.
  • Threat modellingWe enumerate what an attacker would try, then test that it fails. Auth, input validation, rate limits, and signed payloads.
  • Load & failure testingWe push it past expected traffic and cut dependencies on purpose to see how it degrades.
  • ObservabilityStructured logs, metrics, and alerts wired before launch — so a problem is visible before a customer reports it.
  • Reproducible buildsPinned dependencies and containerised environments. It builds the same on your machine as ours.
