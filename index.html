Bakery delivery · production

Bread Crumbs

A bakery delivery application built around three roles — customer, owner, and driver. Real-time ordering, live driver tracking on routed maps, tamper-proof delivery addresses, and race-safe order assignment, built to stay correct under real traffic.

  • Live driver tracking on routed maps
  • Signed delivery addresses — no coordinate spoofing
  • Atomic, race-safe order assignment
  • Real-time updates across all three roles
